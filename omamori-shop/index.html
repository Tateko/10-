    返納

    Henō · Returning old omamori

    After a year, an omamori goes back to a shrine.

    In Japan, old omamori are not thrown away. They are returned to a shrine and burned with thanks in the otakiage ceremony, usually around New Year. Mail yours back to us with any order, or on its own, and we take it to a shrine for you. The return is free with any order.

    • Keep it closeIn a bag, on a keychain, or at your desk. It works best where you carry your wish.
    • Never open itThe paper or wood charm inside is the blessing. Opening the pouch is said to let it out.
    • More than one is fineShrines do not compete. A study omamori and a health omamori can travel together.
    • Say thank youWhen your wish comes true, or after a year, return it with gratitude.

    Questions

    Before you order

    Are these real omamori from the shrine?

    Yes. Every omamori is received in person at the shrine or temple named on the listing, in its original envelope. We never buy from resellers or factories. The photo report and offering receipt show where it came from.

    Is it okay to have someone else receive an omamori for me?

    Yes. In Japan it is common for family members to receive omamori for each other, for example a parent for a child sitting exams. Our visit follows the same custom, called daisan (代参), visiting on someone's behalf.

    How long does delivery take?

    We visit within 7 days of your order and ship within 2 days of the visit. Tracked international shipping takes 3–10 days depending on your country. Most orders arrive within 2–3 weeks.

    Will customs charge me?

    Omamori are small cloth and paper items of low value, and most countries do not charge duty on them. We declare them honestly as religious amulets. Any local import charges are paid by the recipient.

    Can I send it as a gift?

    Yes. Enter the recipient's address at checkout and leave a gift message. We add a card explaining what the omamori is for, and leave out the prices.

    What if the shrine is out of stock?

    Seasonal and limited designs sometimes run out. We message you from the shrine with the alternatives available that day. If none suit you, we refund the item in full.
